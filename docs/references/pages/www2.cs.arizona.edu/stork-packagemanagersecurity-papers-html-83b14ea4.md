## Papers

We have a paper that focuses on an adversary that controls a mirror (or a man-in-the-middle attacker) that appeared at [CCS 2008](http://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2008/). This paper is available as a [PDF](https://www2.cs.arizona.edu/stork/packagemanagersecurity/ccs2008.pdf).

An article discussing the [current state of package manager vulnerabilities](http://www.usenix.org/publications/login/2009-02/openpdfs/samuel.pdf) and what actions an administrator can take appeared in the February 2009 edition of the ;login: magazine.

We released a technical report decribing a broad look at the security of package managers. This is available as University of Arizona Technical Report TR08-02. A longer version is available in Justin Cappos' dissertation

In addition, there are several research papers that describe our research package manager [Stork](http://www.cs.arizona.edu/stork).

- A [Stork Overview paper](http://www.sagecertification.org/events/lisa07/tech/cappos.html) appeared in [LISA 2007](http://www.usenix.org/events/lisa07/).
- A discussion on [Why it is hard to build a long running service](http://www.usenix.org/events/worlds05/tech/cappos.html) appeared in [WORLDS 2005](http://www.usenix.org/events/worlds05/).
-  Proper and its interaction with Stork appeared in a [short version](http://www.usenix.org/events/usenix05/tech/general/muir.html) at [USENIX ATC 2005](http://www.usenix.org/events/usenix05/) and a [full version](http://portal.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=1113375&coll=GUIDE&dl=GUIDE&CFID=56549459&CFTOKEN=88263208&ret=1#Fulltext) in Operating Systems Review 2006.
-  A description of the techniques for centralized management of Stork appeared in ;login: magazine in [the Feb, 2008 edition](http://www.usenix.org/publications/login/2008-02/index.html).
