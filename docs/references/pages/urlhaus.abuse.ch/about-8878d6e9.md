# About

URLhaus is a platform operated by abuse.ch and Spamhaus. The purpose of the platform is to collect, track and share malware URLs, helping network administrators and security analysts to protect their network and customers from cyber threats.

Submissions to URLhaus are shared with security solution providers, antivirus vendors and blocklist providers, including:

- [Spamhaus DBL](https://www.spamhaus.org/dbl/)
- [SURBL](http://www.surbl.org/)
- [Google Safe Browsing (GSB)](https://safebrowsing.google.com/)

If you are a vendor and you would like to use data provided by URLhaus, please have a quick look at the [Terms of Services (ToS)](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/faq/#tos).

Please submit any questions to our partner, Spamhaus Technology, via this contact form:
https://contact.abuse.ch/

URLhaus offers the following feeds:

- Malware URLs (see [API](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/api/))
- API (see [bulk API](https://urlhaus-api.abuse.ch/))
- Malware sample feed (see [bulk API](https://urlhaus-api.abuse.ch/#download-sample))
- [Real time feeds](https://www.spamhaus.com/product/abusech-real-time-threat-intelligence-feeds/), provided by our partner, Spamhaus

## Partnering with Spamhaus

Like abuse.ch, [Spamhaus](https://www.spamhaus.com/) has a mission to strengthen trust and safety on the Internet, operating as an independent organization for over twenty years. This shared vision forms the foundation of our partnership, working together to provide the largest, independently crowdsourced intelligence of tracked malware and botnets.

Spamhaus is recognized as the trusted authority on IP and domain reputation. Globally, they protect 4.5 billion mailboxes and help secure networks against 11.7 million indicators of compromise (IOCs). Together, we build and maintain platforms and datasets to make the internet safer.
